# Rights and third-party sources

## Code

The authors selected MIT for the original study-specific software and its documentation. The licence applies to `code/` and code documentation. Imported packages remain under their respective licences and are installed separately. Local skill implementations, fonts and external QA utilities are not redistributed in this package.

## Data, figures and prompts

The data and figure files are supplied as research source materials. A separate blanket open-data or figure reuse licence has not been assigned in this package. Public accessibility does not transfer ownership of third-party material. The MIT software licence must not be interpreted as licensing the original MIND news content, human neuroimaging data, model weights, or every material from the original study. The prompt materials are reproduced to document the methods.

Original MIND text and impression logs are obtained from [MIND](https://msnews.github.io/) under the dataset provider’s terms. Original news-reading materials are obtained through [OSF CAXFQ](https://doi.org/10.17605/OSF.IO/CAXFQ) under the terms attached to those materials. Model weights must be obtained from their respective providers under their own licences.

The released user/participant labels are the existing coded analysis identifiers, not names or contact details. The compact package contains derived metrics and group maps; individual beta images, article texts and target-level response records are not redistributed here.
